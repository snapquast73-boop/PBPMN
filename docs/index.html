What this page does not do

  • It does not cache a copy of MLB data on a server. The request goes from your browser to statsapi.mlb.com and nowhere else.
  • It does not show a sample score, a placeholder play or a fallback dataset.
  • It does not send anything about you or your session anywhere. The Event log stays in this browser's localStorage.

MLB data remains subject to the notice returned by MLB Advanced Media, L.P. This independent project is not affiliated with or endorsed by Major League Baseball. See data & methodology for exact endpoints, validation rules and limits.
